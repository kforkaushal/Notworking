Our Un-Corporate Manifesto

Where 'networking' means your router is working and 'synergy' is a word you mock.

What is NotWorking?

In a world obsessed with 5 AM miracle mornings and "synergizing paradigms," we offer a well-deserved nap. NotWorking is the anti-LinkedIn. It's a digital sanctuary for the unemployed, the "consultants" between gigs, the artists "finding themselves," and anyone who believes a resume gap is a feature, not a bug. Here, we don't "leverage assets"; we leverage the couch. We don't "climb the ladder"; we question if the ladder is even leaning against the right wall.


Our Core "Features"

We provide revolutionary, paradigm-shifting tools to help you master the art of professional idleness:

The Rant Room: A safe space to scream about "entry-level" jobs that require a personal recommendation from a ghost.
Nap Analytics: Optimize your procrastination by tracking REM cycles to ensure you miss every single "optional" 8 AM Zoom call.
Delusion Dashboard: Quantify your self-deception by tracking "paradigms shifted" and "synergies achieved" while re-watching The Office.
Ghost Counter: Gamify your job search by tracking how many recruiters have ghosted you. Compete with friends for the "Most Invisible Candidate" badge.

What Can You Do Here?

Flaunt your collection of rejection letters like they're Pokémon cards. Endorse your friends for crucial skills like "Binge-Watching," "Advanced Procrastination," and "Writing a Cover Letter Without Crying." Share your proudest moments of doing absolutely nothing and getting away with it. This is your space to be unproductive, unbothered, and unapologetically unemployed.
